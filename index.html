Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Solea

How do I start listing my property with Solea? +

Getting started is simple. Fill out our contact form below or reach out directly via WhatsApp. We'll schedule a free consultation to assess your property, discuss your goals, and create a customized management plan tailored to your needs.

What are the fees or commission structure? +

Our commission structure is transparent and competitive, typically ranging from 20-25% of rental income depending on the level of service required. This covers all management, marketing, guest support, cleaning coordination, and maintenance oversight. We'll provide a detailed breakdown during your consultation.

How do you handle maintenance and emergencies? +

We have a 24/7 rapid response system for emergencies. Our network of trusted local contractors handles routine maintenance and repairs promptly. We conduct regular property inspections and address issues proactively before they become problems. You'll receive detailed reports and photos of all work completed.

What type of properties do you manage? +

We manage a diverse portfolio of short-term rental properties across Malta and Gozo, including apartments, townhouses, villas, and traditional Maltese homes. Whether you have a studio in Sliema or a farmhouse in Gozo, if it meets our quality standards, we'd love to partner with you.

Can I still use my property personally? +

Absolutely! Many of our owners block out specific dates for personal use. We work with you to create a calendar that maximizes rental income while ensuring you can enjoy your property whenever you wish. Simply let us know your preferred dates in advance, and we'll manage the booking schedule accordingly.

How quickly will I see results? +

Most properties are listed and receiving bookings within 2-3 weeks of signing with us. This includes professional photography, listing optimization, and initial marketing. Peak earning potential is typically reached within the first full season as we build your property's reputation and review profile.

What makes Solea different from other property managers? +

Our commitment to "The Standard" sets us apart. We're not just property managers—we're partners in your success. From our 5 Core Standards to our local expertise and personal touch, we treat every property as if it were our own. Our owners benefit from premium marketing, transparent communication, and a team that genuinely cares about maximizing both your income and property value.
